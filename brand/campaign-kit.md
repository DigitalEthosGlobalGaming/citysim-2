# Plotter — Application and launch copy kit

Working materials for review. Check the preferred name, platform rules, features and screenshots before release; the game is a prototype and no release date, price or performance claims have been established.

## Listing copy

**Name:** Plotter  
**Tagline:** One city. Plenty of plans.  
**Short description:** A competitive card-driven city builder where everyone makes their mark on one shared board.

**Long description:**

Welcome to Plotter, a city with room for more than one plan. Pick building cards from a shared market, then place roads, homes, shops and more on a board everyone helps shape.

Look for useful neighbours, build your income and keep an eye on your rivals. Every placement changes the city, and the player with the most points after eight rounds wins. Play against AI opponents or build alongside other players.

**Feature bullets:**

- Share one city: everyone's buildings become part of the same board.
- Make your next move: choose cards and find places that work with the city around them.
- Build your strategy: use building effects, income and scoring opportunities to make your plan pay off.
- Play your way: set up AI opponents or play with other people.
