# Plotter — Messaging

## Core message

Plotter brings players together to build one lively city while each follows a plan of their own.

## Value proposition

Plotter is a competitive city-building game played on a shared board. Choose cards from the market, place buildings alongside everyone's creations and score points for the city you help shape.

## Tagline

**One city. Plenty of plans.** The recommended working line expresses the shared board and the players' different ambitions. It is an invitation, not a rules explanation.

Alternatives: **Make your mark on the map.** (personal contribution); **Every block has a story.** (city life); **Good neighbours. Clever plans.** (warm, tactical); **Build together. Think ahead.** (direct).

## Hierarchy

**Headline:** One city. Plenty of plans.

**Supporting statement:** Pick your cards, find your place and build a city alongside your rivals.

| Message | Proof in the current design |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone builds the same city | Buildings and roads share one board. |
| A good placement changes your plans | Building effects can depend on neighbours, roads and tags. |
| Choose a strategy as you go | Players buy building cards from a shared market and build an income and scoring plan. |
| The city comes alive | Cars and other vehicles animate the roads; traffic is atmospheric rather than a capacity system. |
