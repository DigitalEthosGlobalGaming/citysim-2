# Brand Context

This is a working brief. The name, visual style and creative direction are open to change. Inferences below are hypotheses, not confirmed audience research or decisions.

## Brand
- **Name**: Plotter (preferred working title; availability not yet confirmed)
- **Category**: Competitive city-building and card-driven board game for s&box
- **Description**: Players buy building cards and compete to shape one shared city over eight rounds.
- **Stage**: Prototype; balance still needs human playtesting.
- **Website**: Unknown.

## Audience
- **Primary audience**: Hypothesis: players who enjoy approachable competitive board games, spatial puzzles and short city-building sessions, alone against AI or with friends.
- **Key problem**: Hypothesis: wanting the pleasure of building a lively city and outthinking friends in a short session.
- **Their language**: Not yet researched; do not treat proposed copy as player testimony.

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: A shared city board and simultaneous building combine card choices, spatial competition and a city that comes alive. Matches are designed to last roughly 8–10 minutes.
- **Competitors**: Not yet researched.
- **Market position**: Not yet established.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality words**: Working hypotheses: warm, playful, tactile, clever, welcoming.
- **Tone**: Warm and playful, with clear rules and a competitive edge.
- **Voice admires**: Unknown.

## Values & Mission
- **Core values**: Proposed: clear choices, shared experiences, creative building, lively cities.
- **Mission**: Working draft: Bring people together to build a city full of clever choices and friendly rivalry.

## Goals
- **Primary goal**: Not yet established.
- **Key metrics**: Not yet established.

## Creative boundaries
- The user is open to changing the name, look and feel.
- The user wants warm, playful tabletop city building, and wants to avoid a mobile-game look and generic AI aesthetics.
- The user reviewed the brand preview and chose to keep its visual direction on 1 November 2026. Individual assets and the name remain subject to normal refinement and clearance.
- Existing warm, printed Punchboard presentation is a current implementation, not a fixed brand requirement.
- The brand should reflect the current game; gameplay and balance details may change with playtesting.
