# Naming: Plotter

**Status:** Preferred working title, suggested by the user. Not yet cleared for launch.

## Why it fits

Plotter carries two ideas in one ordinary word: a plot of land on the shared board, and a person planning their next move. It is short, easy to say, and makes room for the game's friendly rivalry without promising a hostile game. A plotting instrument also suggests drawn lines and printed plans, which could inform a tactile tabletop identity.

## Creative direction

- Explore a sturdy wordmark with a simple plot grid or street corner worked into a letter. Keep it readable without the motif.
- Show a city made from individual plots, with player pieces contributing to one shared board.
- Working line: **One city. Plenty of plans.**
- Alternative: **Make your mark on the map.**

## Initial checks — 1 November 2026

- [City Plotter](https://diemer.itch.io/city-plotter) is an existing geography guessing game. It is a different type of game, but the similarity creates a discovery and possible naming conflict worth investigating.
- Plotter is also a common term for a drawing or printing device, which can make generic web searches difficult.
- The project's s&box package identifier remains `degg.city_sim`; its local project display title is now `Plotter`. No package has been published as part of this brand work. A display title and a package identifier are separate decisions to check against platform behaviour.
- A search for the name on s&box did not establish whether an exact listing or identifier is available. Check the platform directly before committing.
- Domain registration and trademark availability were not verified. Automated domain and GitHub checks failed in this environment; ordinary web searches are not a substitute for registry searches. Check relevant jurisdictions and game categories before launch.

No claim of exclusive rights or name availability is made by this preliminary screen.

## Follow-up screen — 1 November 2026

- [City Plotter](https://diemer.itch.io/city-plotter) remains the clearest nearby result: a released geography game, rather than a city builder. Its title and city subject may make search results harder to distinguish; this is not a determination of legal conflict.
- A [2026 US court complaint](https://business.cch.com/ipld/GreenbrierGraphicsSavageInnovationsComplaint20260408041326.pdf) describes a software product line using the name Deed Plotter. This is a party's allegation about a different software category, not an independently verified finding about rights in Plotter for games. It is a useful lead for a proper clearance search.
- `plotter.com` could not be checked conclusively: the authoritative registration-data endpoint was inaccessible from the available tools, and its website returned 403. Neither result establishes ownership or availability.
- Searches of indexed web pages did not establish whether the desired s&box title or identifier is available. A direct platform check is still needed. No USPTO, IP Australia, EUIPO or other official trademark register search was completed, so trademark status remains unknown.

**Decision:** Keep Plotter as the preferred working name, but avoid committing to release assets or registrations on the assumption that it is clear. Check the exact mark and similar marks for games in the markets where the game will be offered, and check the target platform and domain through their authoritative interfaces. Consider specialist clearance if the project is going to market.
