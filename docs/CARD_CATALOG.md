# Current card catalog — 30 October 2026

Implemented v0.12 / balance 2026-10-30.4. [Balance changes and evidence](BALANCE_LOCAL_PLACEMENT_2026-10-30.md). [Sortable CSV](balance/2026-10-30-current-cards.csv).

All 48 cards. Market prices are paid on purchase; placement is free. Limit 0 means unlimited. Fifteen building cards require roads. A road bonus is optional on House, Cafe, School, Museum, Hospital and Tourist Office.

Streets award their owner 1 VP when a new building is placed beside them, once per building per Street owner. There is no recurring Street VP and no retroactive reward for placing roads beside existing buildings.

Parks award their printed base VP when placed, plus 1 VP if beside a Nature card of any owner. Nature neighbours do not stack. Fountain has 2 base VP; other park cards have 1. Sapling awards another 2 VP after its second payout. Lake connects parks. Every park card is one physical tile; there is no size cap or rounding.
